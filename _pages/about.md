I am a Ph.D. candidate at the [Max Planck Institute for Software Systems](https://www.mpi-sws.org/) (MPI-SWS), where I am part of the [Machine Teaching Group](https://machineteaching.mpi-sws.org/) under the supervision of [Prof. Adish Singla](https://machineteaching.mpi-sws.org/adishsingla.html). My research lies at the intersection of generative AI and human-AI interaction. It spans from building and evaluating interactive AI systems for programming and education to developing methods for adapting generative models to individual users. This includes fine-tuning, inference-time personalization, preference learning, agentic pipelines, and automated evaluation and verification. My work combines technical development with large-scale empirical studies of how people interact with these systems and how they affect problem solving and learning.

For my Ph.D., I study how generative AI is reshaping programming and computational problem solving, and how it can make learning more effective, interactive, and personalized. I am particularly interested in the skills that remain crucial and the new ones that become essential as people work with generative AI.

Alongside my academic research, I recently completed a Research Scientist Internship at Amazon, where I worked on retrieval and knowledge curation for agentic AI systems. Before joining MPI-SWS, I worked as a data scientist and software engineer in industry.
